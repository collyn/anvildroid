LXC configuration templates for managed Android runtimes.

config_base, config_3, config_4 and waydroid.seccomp are copied unmodified from
Waydroid 1.6.3 (data/configs/), https://github.com/waydroid/waydroid

License: GPL-3.0-or-later (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html)

AnvilDroid prefers the templates of an installed Waydroid package. These copies
are only used when no Waydroid package (or an incomplete one) is installed, so
Android devices can be prepared and started without installing Waydroid.
